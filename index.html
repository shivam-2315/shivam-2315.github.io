01 — About

My work sits where protocol design meets memory safety — turning specifications into fuzzers, and crashes into patches.

I research system security with a focus on fuzzing for software vulnerability discovery. My current work targets the Linux kernel's network protocol implementations: building grammar-driven, protocol-aware fuzzers that surface memory-safety and concurrency bugs, then driving them through coordinated disclosure to CVE assignment and upstream patches.

Before my PhD I spent over three years at Wipro in ServiceNow development and GRC solutions. I currently teach as a Teaching Assistant for CSE.486 Design Operating Systems.
