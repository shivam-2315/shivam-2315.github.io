About

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at Syracuse University researching system security with a focus on fuzzing for software vulnerability discovery. My current work targets the Linux kernel's network protocol implementations — building grammar-driven, protocol-aware fuzzers to surface memory-safety and concurrency bugs, then driving them through coordinated disclosure to CVE assignment and upstream patches.

Before my PhD, I spent over three years in industry at Wipro working on ServiceNow development and GRC solutions. I also teach as a Teaching Assistant for CSE.486 Design Operating Systems.
